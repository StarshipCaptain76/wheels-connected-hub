# Just Wheels Hessequa

**Southern Cape car club** for classics, hot rods, bakkies, bikes and anything else with wheels — based in **Hessequa** (Riversdale, Stilbaai, Heidelberg, Albertinia, Gouritsmond, Witsand) and rolling the **Garden Route** and **Klein Karoo**.

If it’s got wheels, it belongs here.

**Live site:** [www.justwheels.co.za](https://www.justwheels.co.za)

## Built in the garage, not the boardroom

Just Wheels Hessequa started with one WhatsApp on **14 November 2016 at 20:22**. A handful of okes, a few cold drinks, photos of cars — and before anyone took minutes, a club was rolling.

Oom **Johan Beetge** got the wheels turning in Brakpan, then packed the passion coastward to **Stilbaai**. The first get-together down here was at the **Lappiesbaai** car park. Today we’re still one of the most active motoring groups on the Southern Cape — more garage than boardroom.

Members range from concours-quality restorers to Saturday-morning scrapyard hunters. No egos, no snobbery. Breakfast runs, show-and-shines, workshop days, braais, and stories that get better with every kilometre.

The club is **English and Afrikaans**. Membership is **free**.

## What’s on the site

### [Club events](https://www.justwheels.co.za/events)

Monthly **breakfast runs**, **show-and-shines**, road trips and workshop days across the Southern Cape. Upcoming meets, maps, RSVP, and **Concours Mini** voting when a show is live. Add the run to your calendar and get directions from your phone.

### [Marketplace](https://www.justwheels.co.za/classifieds)

Cars, parts, memorabilia and gear for sale by club members around Riversdale, Stilbaai and the Garden Route. Call or WhatsApp the seller. Members can post their own listings.

### [Photo gallery](https://www.justwheels.co.za/gallery)

Shots from runs, shows and workshop days. Tag your mates. Relive the Lappiesbaai parking-lot energy.

### [Club shop](https://www.justwheels.co.za/shop)

Caps, tees, patches, stickers and the rest. Rep the tribe. Enquire online — pickup and payment the old-fashioned way.

### [Sponsors](https://www.justwheels.co.za/sponsors)

The local businesses that keep us on the road. Want your workshop, parts shop or diner in front of the tribe? Apply on the site.

### Members area

Sign in and you get:

- A **digital membership card** that works offline at the gate
- Member directory and garage (what you drive)
- Event invites, photo tags and push notifications
- Newsletter editions
- Your own classifieds

## Local development

Node.js + npm.

```sh
git clone https://github.com/StarshipCaptain76/wheels-connected-hub.git
cd wheels-connected-hub
npm i
npm run dev
```

Stack: TanStack Start, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Supabase.

The public site is English / Afrikaans, installable as an app, and indexed only on `www.justwheels.co.za`.
